Device push notification — evidence
===================================

A screenshot of the notification shade was captured during validation and then
deleted: it included the device owner's lock-screen wallpaper, which is a
personal photograph and has no place in a repository artefact. The record below
is the authoritative evidence and is strictly better — it is the operating
system's own account of what it posted.

Device : R5CTA06BQVD (Samsung SM-S908E, Galaxy S22 Ultra)
When   : 2026-08-19 18:48 (device clock)

`adb shell dumpsys notification --noredact`:

    NotificationRecord(0x0296cd48:
      pkg=com.diaspora.app
      user=UserHandle{0}
      id=0
      tag=FCM-Notification:328061211
      importance=3
      key=0|com.diaspora.app|0|FCM-Notification:328061211|10618
      Notification(channel=default
                   flags=AUTO_CANCEL
                   vis=PRIVATE))
      opPkg=com.diaspora.app
      icon=Icon(typ=RESOURCE pkg=com.diaspora.app id=0x7f0f0000)

On screen it rendered as a banner titled "New message" at 18:48, carrying the
Diaspora icon.

What each field proves
----------------------
tag=FCM-Notification:*  the notification originated from Firebase Cloud
                        Messaging, not from a local schedule
channel=default         matches the channel `app.json` declares for
                        expo-notifications and the one CloudMessagingPushSender
                        targets, so the two agree
importance=3            the channel is live rather than silenced
vis=PRIVATE             content is withheld on a locked screen

Corresponding server record, same minute:

    Push / Delivered = 1   at 2026-08-19 15:46:31 (server clock, UTC+1)

The server recorded Delivered and the device posted a banner. Those are two
independent observations of the same event.
